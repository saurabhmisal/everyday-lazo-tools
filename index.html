Set up your workspace

01

Get Everyday

Sign up with your email and enter the verification code. Your Lazo account shows available Mac downloads. We’ll email you when a new Everyday release is ready.

02

Choose your data folder

Set a recovery password at first launch, then select Choose folder & start. Pick a location on a local drive for your tasks and notes. Keep the password in your password manager.

03

Keep a recovery copy

Use Save encrypted backup and keep the file on a separate drive. Back up linked files and external notes separately.

Reinstalling Everyday?

Reinstall the app, choose Open existing data folder and select your original Lazo Everyday folder.

If you deleted that folder too, choose Restore backup file and enter your backup password.
Reconnecting sources brings back accessible originals. Everyday-only tasks, drafts and focus history require the folder or a backup.
